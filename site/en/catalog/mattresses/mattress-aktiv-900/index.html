Anatomical double—sided mattress "AKTIV" is a mattress with a block of independent ANTINOISE springs in combination with highly elastic polyurethane foam. A distinctive feature of the mattress is 2 options for the rigidity of the sides. The mattress "Asset" is a good choice for those who appreciate a rational approach to comfort. Spring block. The mattress is based on a block of independent ANTINOISE springs, manufactured on the latest high-tech, fully automated equipment from one of the world's leading manufacturers.

The ANTINOISE spring block provides anatomical support for every area of your body. The springs are of medium stiffness, 190 mm high, packed in separate bags with a height of 95 mm, are compressed under pressure, providing the necessary reserve of elasticity and rigidity. Each spring is compressed independently of the others. Such a spring block provides point elasticity and body support corresponding to the load. As an insulating layer between the spring block and the mattress fillers, a high-density "SPUNBOND-PROTECT" material is used, which reduces friction between the flooring layers and the spring block in the mattress, protecting the product from wear. Soft fillers. On one side of the mattress, a highly elastic polyurethane foam with a height of 20 mm is used.

The surface of the product on this side is medium hard. On the other hand, a highly elastic polyurethane foam with a height of 24 mm, made with a wave according to a unique cutting system, creates a massage and "stretching" effect of the spine. Due to this, the spine relaxes as much as possible in all departments and stretches. On this side, the mattress has the most optimal, average degree of rigidity. Due to the different rigidity of the sides, you can choose a surface for sleeping based on your well-being and mood. The polyurethane foam used for the manufacture of the mattress has a CertiPUR certificate of conformity. It testifies to the environmental friendliness and safety of products. In the specialized testing laboratory of the Republic of Belarus, the mattress has been tested for the durability of springs and shrinkage of soft elements. According to the test results, there is a declaration of conformity of the product to safety standards.


The double-sided mattress withstands significant loads, supports the spine in the correct position, and also relieves stress from the spine and joints. Case. The double-sided cover with the "Winter—Summer" function is made using two types of fabrics: COMFORT GREY fabric - light and smooth. It absorbs moisture well, has good thermal conductivity, so it will be comfortable to rest on it in the hot summer season. COMFORT WHITE fabric is a soft knitted fabric. It is pleasant to the touch, warms well in the cold season, has antistatic properties. It has a beautiful appearance. The mattress cover is removable, with a zipper. Retains its original properties after washing. It fits as tightly as possible to the surface of the mattress and protects it from all sides, prolonging the service life and maintaining the hygiene of the sleeping place. With the "AKTIV" mattress, a good dream will become a reality!
