The Memory Foam 180×200 cm topper by VEGAS is designed for those who need maximum comfort and space for a king-size bed. This size provides a full sleeping surface for two or even three people, measuring 180 cm width × 200 cm length × 5 cm height. It offers full body support, even pressure distribution, and maximum relaxation. Ideal for large families, couples, or luxury bedrooms.


The topper features a 50 mm high-elasticity Memory Foam layer, fully adapting to body contours, reducing pressure points, and promoting muscle relaxation during sleep. The top layer has a Wave contour cut, providing softness, natural movement, improving blood circulation, and relaxing the nervous system. The material is anti-static, resistant to pilling, and ensures full hygiene.


The removable zip cover allows easy washing or dry cleaning. Elastic corner attachments keep the topper securely in place. It also features a Winter–Summer function, providing comfort in all seasons.


VEGAS — 29 years of expertise:

Founded in 1997 in Belarus, VEGAS is an international brand, producing high-quality mattresses, Memory Foam toppers, and bedroom accessories. The company uses European materials, including coconut coir from the Netherlands, latex from Belgium, polyurethane from the UK, and German springs. All products are certified to international standards, ensuring quality, safety, and durability.


The topper provides thermal insulation, protects the mattress from moisture and dirt, and extends its lifespan. Perfect for use with Memory Foam mattresses or warm blankets, maximizing sleep comfort. Suitable for naps, guest rooms, or family use.


Key benefits:

 • Full anatomical support across the body

 • High quality and durability

 • Safe, hypoallergenic materials

 • Easy maintenance and washing

 • Multi-season functionality
