The Memory Foam 140×200 cm topper by VEGAS is designed for those who need personal comfort for a double or family bed. This size provides full rest for two people while not taking up excessive space, making it ideal for small to medium bedrooms. Measuring 140 cm width × 200 cm length × 5 cm height, it provides natural comfort, fully adapting to body contours and evenly distributing pressure points.

The topper features a 50 mm high-elasticity Memory Foam layer, which quickly conforms to the body, providing maximum relaxation during sleep. The top layer has a Wave contour cut, adding softness, promoting nervous system relaxation, and improving blood circulation. The material is anti-static, resistant to pilling, and ensures hygiene.

The removable zip cover allows easy machine washing or dry cleaning. Elastic corner attachments keep the topper securely in place. It also features a Winter–Summer function, providing comfort year-round.

VEGAS — 29 years of expertise:
Founded in 1997 in Belarus, VEGAS is an international brand, producing high-quality mattresses, Memory Foam toppers, and bedroom accessories. The company uses European materials, including coconut coir from the Netherlands, latex from Belgium, polyurethane from the UK, and German springs. All products are certified to international standards, ensuring safety, durability, and quality.

The topper provides thermal insulation, protects the mattress from moisture and dirt, and extends its lifespan. Perfect for use with Memory Foam mattresses or warm blankets, maximizing sleep comfort. Suitable for naps, guest rooms, or family use.
