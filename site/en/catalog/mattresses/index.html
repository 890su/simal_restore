Mattresses in Tbilisi, Batumi, and throughout Georgia.

The mattresses presented on our website belong to the premium class and are characterized by innovative technologies, high-quality materials, and thoughtful design. They are created to ensure maximum comfort and body support during sleep, which contributes to quality rest and recovery.

Purchasing a mattress of Belarusian production in Georgia is easy in cities like Tbilisi and Batumi through our stores, and in other regions of Georgia via delivery. Contact our company "Simal," and we will offer you the necessary product according to your preferences.

One of the key features of our mattresses is the use of an independent spring unit, which provides anatomical support for each zone of the body. The springs are highly elastic and compress individually in separate casings, ensuring the necessary elasticity and strength. Thanks to this, the spring unit creates point support for the body even under very high constant loads, ensuring the most natural position of the spine. Orthopedic mattresses with individual springs do not creak or sag, as is common with low-segment price mattresses.

Elastic fillers also play an important role in ensuring comfort. Some models use a layer of coconut coir and natural latex, as well as the modern material Memory Foam, which gives one side of the mattress additional firmness, and the other side lightness and extra comfort. In some mattress models, high-resilience polyurethane foam is used, manufactured using a unique "wavy" cutting system that creates a massage effect and promotes body relaxation.

Typically, our mattresses have asymmetrical sides, so you can choose the perfect comfort option!

Mattress production takes place on high-tech equipment, where precision and modern materials are transformed into a high-tech product on which you will enjoy spending your nights.

     

The Simal mattress store offers products with different comfort zones. For example, the "Imperial" mattress has 14 zones that evenly distribute the load across the entire surface, provide maximum body support, and eliminate the need to frequently change sleeping positions. Additional firmness zones, formed by a unique zonal cutting system, ensure the natural alignment of the spine.

Mattress covers are made considering modern technologies and trends. We offer premium, removable "winter-summer" covers. Some models are equipped with an additional ventilation system – a 3D aerator around the mattress perimeter, which promotes effective air circulation within the product. This protects the mattress from dust accumulation and excess moisture, creating and maintaining the necessary microclimate for quality sleep.

Various materials are used for the covers, including fabrics characterized by lightness and cleanliness, elasticity and durability, as well as fabrics that are flame-retardant from a smoldering cigarette. These materials are breathable and ensure comfortable sleeping conditions in different seasons. The high-resilience polyurethane foam used in mattress production has a CertiPUR compliance certificate, confirming the product's eco-friendliness and safety. Furthermore, our mattresses undergo rigorous testing for durability and compliance with safety standards, confirmed by relevant certificates and declarations. This ensures the longevity and high quality of our products.

Ordering a mattress in Batumi or Tbilisi is now easy, as we have stores in these cities, and we also deliver products to other cities in Georgia! Call and visit our stores!
