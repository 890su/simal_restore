Today, when choosing the arrangement of a room, apartment or house, the buyer very often pays attention to such a piece of furniture as a chest of drawers. This is a practical to use and convenient element of cabinet furniture in all respects. Many people choose it instead of a cabinet because of its size and practicality, a huge selection of standard sizes and different designs, as well as the availability of several colors. You can place or put various things on the chest of drawers, use it as a TV stand, or hang a mirror above it.
 
   You can buy a chest of drawers in Batumi, Kutaisi, Tbilisi and other cities from our company.

If you are looking for chests of drawers similar to products from the Swedish manufacturer IKEA, then look at the products presented on our website in the appropriate section, since the cabinets for things sold by our company are manufactured according to the standards of the Swedish concern. The chests of drawers have a minimalistic design that is suitable for all design solutions of residential and commercial spaces. The main color is white, but on request the products can also be supplied in bleached oak and wenge. Also here you will find wardrobes, bedside tables, shelves of various configurations, as well as beds and sofas.


   The chests of drawers presented in our store have a number of advantages:
  • Modern materials with light-resistant coating
  • High-quality silent fittings
  • Packaging that allows you to transport chests of drawers over long distances without mechanical damage
  • Clear and easy assembly, simple instructions included in each product.
